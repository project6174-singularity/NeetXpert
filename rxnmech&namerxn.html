The 4-question engine

01

Who attacks?
Electrophile seeks electrons; nucleophile donates an electron pair; radicals react through single-electron pathways.

02

Where?
Look for electron-rich π bonds, electrophilic carbonyl carbon, or a carbon attached to a leaving group.

03

What intermediate?
Carbocation, carbanion, radical, halonium ion, alkoxide or sigma complex can decide the route.

04

Why this product?
Use resonance, substitution, inductive effects, sterics, leaving-group ability and aromaticity.

NEET move: mechanism → intermediate → stability → major product. Only then reach for the memorised name.

Micro-glossary

CORE INSTINCTS
Electrophile

Electron-pair seeker; attacks electron-rich regions.

Nucleophile

Electron-pair donor; attacks an electron-poor centre.

Leaving group

Departs with the bonding electron pair.

Carbocation

Electron-deficient carbon; stability controls many pathways.

Carbanion

Electron-rich carbon; resonance and substituents matter.

Resonance

Delocalisation of electrons across a conjugated system.

Markovnikov

Common ionic HX addition orientation based on the more stable carbocation pathway.

Anti-Markovnikov

Peroxide-assisted HBr addition via a radical pathway.
